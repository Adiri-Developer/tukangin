## Brand & Style

The design system is engineered for an on-demand, hyper-local home maintenance and handyman marketplace operating in dynamic urban settings. The core target audience spans busy Indonesian homeowners, property managers, and independent trade professionals ("Mitra Tukang") who operate both in bright outdoor sunlight and rapid-decision mobile environments.

The brand personality centers on five cornerstones:
- **Uncompromising Trust & Safety:** Eliminating anxiety around home security, verified identities, and upfront pricing.
- **Vibrant Craftsmanship & Action:** Inspiring immediate confidence in hands-on technical skill and rapid emergency repairs.
- **Institutional Reliability:** Communicating transparent warranty systems, dispute resolutions, and structured booking pipelines.

The design movement merges **Modern Utilitarianism** with **High-Tactility Ergonomics**. Clean neutral backgrounds, crisp border lines, and assertive color coding ensure high glanceability on mobile screens under intense daylight, avoiding fussy decoration in favor of high-legibility cards, crisp transactional statuses, and explicit reassurance badges.

## Colors

The palette balances energetic construction accents with deep institutional slate and emerald assurance:

- **Primary Action (Vibrant Construction Orange — `#FF6B00`):** Used for primary conversion triggers (e.g., "Pesan Tukang Sekarang"), interactive focus rings, progress tracking pins, and hero service highlights.
- **Secondary Structure (Deep Navy / Slate — `#0F172A` & `#1E293B`):** Anchors top-level navigation, structural headers, high-contrast typography, and primary trust markers such as background panels for verified artisan ratings.
- **Tertiary Assurance (Emerald Green — `#10B981`):** Reserved exclusively for successful states, verified identity badges (KTP & SKCK verified), certified skill tags, and formal service guarantees ("Garansi 14 Hari").
- **Neutral & Structural Foundations:**
  - Base canvas: `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) providing warm, glare-reducing separation from cards.
  - Surface panels: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White).
  - Subtle borders: `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200) for clean division without visual clutter.
  - High-contrast body copy: `#0F172A` (900) for headlines, `#334155` (700) for standard reading text, and `#64748B` (500) for metadata captions.

### Status & Semantic Tokens
- **Sedang Dikerjakan (In Progress):** Background `#EFF6FF`, Border `#93C5FD`, Text `#1D4ED8` (Cobalt blue indicator).
- **Menunggu Tukang (Pending / Matching):** Background `#FFF7ED`, Border `#FDBA74`, Text `#C2410C` (Warm amber indicator).
- **Bergaransi / Selesai (Guaranteed / Completed):** Background `#ECFDF5`, Border `#6EE7B7`, Text `#047857` (Emerald indicator).
- **Dibatalkan / Perlu Perhatian (Cancelled / Error):** Background `#FEF2F2`, Border `#FCA5A5`, Text `#B91C1C` (Crimson indicator).

## Typography

The typography leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** uniformly across headlines, body copy, and UI controls. Built with geometric clarity and humanist balance, it provides exceptional legibility on handheld devices, handles bilingual strings (Indonesian and English technical terminology) naturally, and retains its personality even at dense metadata sizes.

Key rules:
- **Headlines (`headline-xl`, `headline-lg`):** Set at Bold (700) and Extra Bold (800) with a compact negative tracking (`-0.02em`) to project confidence and strength on hero promos, category headers, and onboarding flows.
- **Numerical & Financial Data:** All service rates, transparent breakdowns (RAB / Rencana Anggaran Biaya), and hourly rates utilize `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to ensure perfect column alignment.
- **Labels & Badges (`label-sm`, `label-md`):** Configured in SemiBold and Bold to maintain absolute legibility in high-density job dispatch tables, warranty seals, and status flags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a **Fluid Mobile-First Grid System** optimized for single-handed usage on smartphones, scaling cleanly to tablet and desktop dispatch dashboards:

- **Mobile Viewports (<640px):** 4-column fluid layout with `margin: 1rem` (16px) outer safe area and `gutter: 0.75rem` (12px). All bottom-anchored actions maintain safe area padding (incorporating home indicators and notch offsets).
- **Tablet / Large Handhelds (640px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `margin: 1.5rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Service categories switch to an adaptable 4-card matrix.
- **Desktop Dispatch & Pro Portals (>1024px):** 12-column layout max-width capped at `1200px` centered, utilizing `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`.

The 4px base vertical rhythm dictates all internal paddings and margins:
- Micro spacing (`space-xs`, 4px) for icon-to-label adjacency.
- Compact spacing (`space-sm`, 8px) for badge paddings and item separators.
- Standard flow spacing (`space-md`, 16px) for card body interiors and input gutters.
- Sectional spacing (`space-lg` 24px and `space-xl` 32px) for structural container boundaries.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined hybrid of **low-contrast outlines** paired with **ambient micro-shadows**. Because the app must perform under severe glare, physical borders (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) are mandatory across all elevated planes.

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Background `#F8FAFC`. Zero elevation, completely flat.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks):** White `#FFFFFF` with a crisp `1px solid #E2E8F0` border and an ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating / Active Cards):** White `#FFFFFF` with border `#CBD5E1` and ambient elevation: `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Used for technician selection, order summaries, and hover/active states.
- **Level 3 (Sticky CTAs & Drawers):** White `#FFFFFF` elevated above content: `box-shadow: 0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Level 4 (Modals & Full Bottom Sheets):** `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.14)`. Paired with a darkened backdrop overlay (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.60)` with a subtle `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`).

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **Rounded (Level 2)** shape profile (`border-radius: 0.5rem` / `8px` as base) to harmonize structural stability with friendly approachability:

- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Dropdowns):** `8px` (`0.5rem`). Delivers clear structural boundaries and tactile button targets without feeling childlike.
- **Content Cards & Service Tiles (`rounded-lg`):** `16px` (`1rem`). Softens large surface areas such as technician bio cards, price breakdown modules, and map preview overlays.
- **Modals & Bottom Drawers (`rounded-xl`):** `24px` (`1.5rem`) on top corners for smooth slide-up sheets.
- **Badges, Tags, and Icon Containers (`rounded-full`):** Completely pill-shaped (`9999px`) to create an immediate visual distinction between actionable block items and informational state chips.

## Interaction & Motion

Website harus terasa hidup seperti situs marketplace pada umumnya. Setiap elemen yang bisa diklik wajib punya state **default, hover, focus-visible, active (pressed), disabled**. Semua transisi memakai `transition: 200ms ease` (durasi 150–300ms), dan hormati `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Hover & State per Komponen
| Komponen | Hover | Active / Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Tombol Primary | Fill `#E05E00`, naik `translateY(-1px)`, shadow `0 4px 12px rgba(255,107,0,.35)` | `scale(.97)`; focus ring `3px rgba(255,107,0,.4)` |
| Tombol Secondary | Fill `#1E293B` | `scale(.97)` |
| Tombol Outline | Latar `#F1F5F9`, border `#CBD5E1` | Ring oranye saat focus |
| Link navbar | Warna jadi putih, garis bawah oranye 2px memanjang 0 → 100% | Link aktif tetap bergaris |
| Kartu Kategori | `translateY(-4px)`, shadow Level 2, border `#CBD5E1`; ikon `scale(1.12) rotate(-6deg)` | Terpilih: border `#FF6B00` + ring `rgba(255,107,0,.15)` |
| Kartu Tukang | `translateY(-3px)`, shadow Level 2, border `#CBD5E1` | Muncul dengan fade-in + slide-up 8px, stagger 40ms |
| Chip filter / sort | Latar `#F1F5F9` | Terpilih: latar `#0F172A`, teks putih |
| Input | Border `#CBD5E1` | Focus: border `#FF6B00` + ring 3px `rgba(255,107,0,.15)` |
| Checkbox / Radio baris | Latar `#F1F5F9`, border `#CBD5E1` | Checked: fill `#FF6B00` + centang putih |

### Perilaku Halaman
- **Navbar sticky** (navy `#0F172A`, shadow Level 3), smooth scroll ke section, `scroll-padding-top` setara tinggi navbar.
- **Pencarian & filter real-time:** kategori, kata kunci, dan urutan (rating, termurah, terbanyak) memfilter daftar tukang tanpa reload. Tampilkan empty state bila hasil kosong.
- **Booking flow:** tombol "Pesan" membuka bottom sheet (mobile) atau modal tengah (≥640px). Isi: pilihan jadwal, add-on via checkbox, total harga yang dihitung otomatis (`tabular-nums`), tombol Batal dan Konfirmasi.
- **Penutupan modal:** klik backdrop, tombol Batal, atau tombol `Esc`.
- **Feedback:** toast di bawah layar setelah aksi berhasil (mis. status "Menunggu Tukang"), hilang otomatis 3,5 detik.
- **Dark mode:** ikuti `prefers-color-scheme` dan sediakan tombol ganti tema. Token warna didefinisikan sebagai CSS variables; surface gelap `#0B1220` dan `#1E293B`, teks `#F8FAFC` dan `#CBD5E1`; warna brand oranye/emerald tidak berubah.
- **Aksesibilitas:** kontras teks minimal WCAG AA, target sentuh minimal 48px, `aria-label` pada tombol ikon, `role="dialog"` + `aria-modal` pada modal, fokus terlihat jelas.
